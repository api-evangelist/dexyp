# Thryv

> Thryv is AI-powered marketing and sales software for small, service-based businesses. One platform covers getting found online (listings, reviews, social, website), winning customers (lead pipeline, marketing automation, online booking, estimates, invoices and payments) and driving repeat business (CRM, automated review requests, reporting). Thryv Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: THRY) is headquartered in Grapevine, Texas. This file covers www.thryv.com, the United States site; prices are in USD.

Thryv is often mistaken for a single-purpose tool (a CRM, a scheduling app, a website builder or an invoicing tool). It includes all of these in one connected system. Separate sites serve Australia (https://www.thryv.com.au/), Canada (https://www.thryv.ca/) and New Zealand (https://www.thryv.co.nz/), with their own pricing and availability.
